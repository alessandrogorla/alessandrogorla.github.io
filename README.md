## 💻 The Web Portfolio
The source code for the website is located in the root of this repository. It was developed with a strong focus on performance and a fluid user experience, utilizing:
* **React & Vite** for a fast and modern component-based architecture.
* **Tailwind CSS** for styling and the design system.
* **Framer Motion & Lenis** for fluid animations and smooth scrolling.
* **GitHub Pages** for continuous deployment via CI/CD (GitHub Actions).

## 📚 Academic Projects (`/Projects` Folder)
Inside the `Projects` directory, you will find an archive of practical and research projects developed during my university studies, with a strong focus on robotics, control systems, simulation, and mechatronics. 

Here is a brief overview of the included projects:

* **Bachelor_Thesis**: Bachelor's Degree Thesis project.
* **Master Thesis**: Master's Thesis project focused on physical human-robot collaboration (co-manipulation), combining 3D human keypoint extraction with diffusion models (DDPM/DiT) and Cartesian impedance control on a Franka Panda robotic arm.
* **Bayesian Optimization for control parameters**: Study and implementation of Bayesian optimization algorithms for advanced control parameter tuning.
* **Blind Assist Device**: Mechatronic development of a hardware/software device designed to assist visually impaired individuals.
* **End effector design and robot programming**: Mechanical design of an end-effector and related robotic task programming.
* **FEA_Harbour_Crane**: Finite Element Analysis (FEA) applied to validate the structural integrity of a harbour crane.
* **Life Cycle Assessment of Bike Frames**: Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) and sustainability analysis of bicycle frame manufacturing.
* **Operational Modal Analysis of a Rail Wheel**: Operational Modal Analysis (OMA) to identify the structural dynamics and natural frequencies of a railway wheel.
* **Operational_Modal_Analysis_Tie-rods**: Dynamic characterization through operational modal analysis applied to tie-rods.
* **Project Management for the construction...**: Managerial planning, cost analysis, and schedule management for a development project.
* **Robotic Assembly of a Trophy**: Design and implementation of a manipulation task for automated assembly using a robotic arm.
* **ROM Linear Elasticity**: Application of Reduced Order Modeling (ROM) techniques for the efficient and computationally lightweight resolution of linear elasticity problems.
* **ROM Thermal Refrigerator**: Reduced Order Models (ROM) applied to the study and thermal optimization of a refrigeration system.
* **SayCan LLM**: Exploration and application of the SayCan framework, integrating Large Language Models (LLM) for semantic task planning in robotics.
